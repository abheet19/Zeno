## Conditions carried from the recommendation — applied as defaults

1. **Compound wordmark for shipped identity.** Ship "Zeno Forge", "Zeno Command" etc.; avoid bare "Zeno" as the public mark. Grade: HIGH — four live/pending US Class 42 ZENOs, including PRONTO.AI's plain word mark in AI/ML SaaS and a Class 9 recital covering unqualified "downloadable computer software development tools".
2. **Scoped packages only.** `@abheet19/zeno-*`. **Never** publish or claim the bare `zeno` name on any registry. `crates.io/zeno` alone has ~10.4M downloads.
3. **"Zeno Link" is renamed to "Zeno Mesh".** Eclipse **zenoh** is pronounced identically *and is a pub/sub linking protocol in the same Rust/robotics ecosystem* — "Zeno Link" would have been a homophone of a real competitor product inside that sub-brand's own category. "Mesh" also matches §5.6's own language ("private device mesh"). Alternates if the owner prefers: **Zeno Relay**, **Zeno Tether**, **Zeno Reach**.
4. **The Class 042 exposure goes to a lawyer first**, before any filing, domain purchase, public launch or app-store submission. Nothing in Phase 0 does any of those.

## Standing caveats (flagged once, not re-asked)

- **Suffix collisions retained deliberately.** *Vault* (HashiCorp), *Forge* (Autodesk APS / SourceForge) and *Glass* (Google Glass) are existing major product names. Compounding with a distinctive stem is the normal, defensible practice, but expect "Zeno Vault" to be shadowed by HashiCorp in developer search. Owner may rename any of the three at any point before Gate 1 without disturbing anything else.
- **Preliminary evidence, never clearance.** No trademark clearance has been performed. Register evidence came from TMview's public API; four national front-ends are CAPTCHA-gated and were not bypassed.
- **Retired names remain retired:** INSHERON, Aurethos. Never proposed, never restored from imported history.
