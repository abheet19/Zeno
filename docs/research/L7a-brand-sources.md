# L7a — Brand Candidate Source Verification & Product-Fit Analysis

**Program:** Personal AI assistant family — brand character "calm, exact, candid, deliberate"; promise **"Reason before action."**
**Worker:** Phase-0A research worker L7a (read-only public web research). **Date of all access:** 2026-08-24.
**Scope:** Classical verification, product-signal fit, pronunciation/speech desk analysis, cross-language negatives, cultural-fit risks for exactly six candidates: ZENO, SENECA, CATO, ARISTO, ATHENA, ZEUS.
**Out of scope (sibling worker):** trademark/collision clearance; final ranking (fan-in).

**Claim labels:** `[verified]` = confirmed against a cited URL on 2026-08-24 · `[inferred]` = desk analysis / standard reference knowledge not re-verified against a live URL · `[unknown]` = could not be established from accessible sources.

**Note on classification (verified, not assumed):** Zeno of Citium, Seneca the Younger, and Aristo of Chios are confirmed Stoic philosophers; Cato the Younger is confirmed as a Stoic-practicing *statesman*, not a philosopher of the school; Athena and Zeus are confirmed Greek-mythological deities. Details and citations per name below.

**Wake-word baseline used throughout (verified):** Voice-industry guidance converges on a minimum of ~3 syllables for a wake phrase (3–4 including any intro word) to avoid false accepts, with plosive/affricate onsets (/k/, /t/, /d/, /g/) preferred for clear acoustic onsets. Sources: SoundHound "5 Keys to Better Wake Word Design" (https://www.soundhound.com/blog/5-keys-to-better-wake-word-design/), Sensory 2026 custom-wake-word guide (https://sensory.com/custom-wake-words-branded-voice-ux-guide-2026/), FutureBeeAI wake-word length note (https://www.futurebeeai.com/knowledge-hub/ideal-wake-word-length). Accessed via search summary 2026-08-24.
